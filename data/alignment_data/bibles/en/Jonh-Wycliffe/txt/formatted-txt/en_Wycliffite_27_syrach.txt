1:1 Al wisdom is of the Lord God, and was euere with hym, and is bifore the world.
1:2 Who noumbride the grauel of the see, and the dropis of reyn, and the daies of the world? Who mesuride the hiynesse of heuene, and the breed of erthe, and the depthe of the see?
1:3 Who enserchide the wisdom of God, that goith bifore alle thingis?
1:4 Wisdom was formed firste of alle thingis, and the vndurstonding of prudence, fro the world.
1:5 The welle of wisdom is the sone of God in hiy thingis; and the entryng of that wisdom is euerlastynge comaundementis.
1:6 To whom was the roote of wisdom schewid? and who knewe the sutilites therof?
1:7 To whom was the lore of wisdom shewid, and maad opyn? and who vndurstood the multipliyng of the entryng therof?
1:8 Oon is the hiyeste creatour of alle thingis, almyyti, and a myyti kyng, and worthi to be dred ful miche, sittynge on the trone of that wisdom, and God hauynge lordschipe.
1:9 He fourmyde `that wisdom in the Hooli Ghost, and he siy, and noumbride, and he mesuride.
1:10 And he schedde out it on alle hise werkis, and on ech fleisch bi his yifte; he yyueth it to hem that louen hym.
1:11 The drede of the Lord is glorie, and gloriyng, and gladnesse, and a coroun of ful out ioiyng.
1:12 The drede of the Lord schal delite the herte; and schal yyue gladnesse and ioie in to lengthe of daies.
1:13 To hym that dredith God, it schal be wel in the laste thingis; and he schal be blessid in the dai of his deth.
1:14 Forsothe thei to whiche wisdom apperith in siyt, louen it in siyt, and in knowyng of hise grete thingis.
1:15 The loue of God is onourable wisdom.
1:16 The bigynnyng of wisdom is the drede of the Lord; and it is formyd togidere in the wombe with feithful men, and it goith with chosun wymmen, and is knowun with iust men and feithful.
1:17 The drede of the Lord is religiouste of kunnyng.
1:18 Religiouste schal kepe, and schal iustifie the herte; and schal yyue myrthe and ioie.
1:19 It schal be wel to hym that dredith God; and he schal be blessid in the daies of his coumfort.
1:20 The fulnesse of wisdom is for to drede God; and fulnesse is of the fruytis therof.
1:21 It schal fille ech yifte of hym of generaciouns, and reseitis of the tresouris therof.
1:22 The coroun of wisdom is the drede of the Lord, and fillith pees, and the fruyt of heelthe.
1:23 And he siy, and noumbride it; forsothe euer eithir ben the yiftis of God.
1:24 Wisdom schal departe the kunnyng and vndurstondyng of prudence; and it enhaunsith the glorie of hem, that holden it.
1:25 The roote of wisdom is for to drede God; forsothe the braunchis therof ben longe durynge.
1:26 Vndurstonding, and religiouste of kunnyng ben in the tresouris of wisdom; but wisdom is abhomynacioun to synners.
1:27 The drede of the Lord puttith awei synne,
1:28 for he that is with out drede, mai not be iustified; for whi the wrathfulnesse of his pride is the destriyng of hym.
1:29 A pacient man schal suffre til in to tyme; and aftirward schal be yelding of mirthe.
1:30 Good wit schal hide the wordis of hym til in to a tyme; and the lippis of many men schulen telle out the wit of hym.
1:31 In the tresouris of wisdom is signefiyng of kunnyng;
1:32 but the worschipyng of God is abhomynacioun to a synnere.
1:33 A! sone, coueitynge wisdom, kepe thou riytfulnesse, and God schal yyue it to thee.
1:34 For whi the drede of the Lord is wisdom, and kunnyng, and that
1:35 that is wel plesaunt to hym is feith and myldenesse; and God schal fille the tressours of hym.
1:36 Be thou not rebel, and vnbileueful to the drede of the Lord; and neiye thou not to hym in double herte.
1:37 Be thou not an ypocrite in the siyt of men; and be thou not sclaundrid in thi lippis.
1:38 Take thou kepe to tho, lest thou falle, and brynge disonour to thi soule; and lest God schewe thi priuytees,
1:39 and hurtle thee doun in the myddis of the synagoge;
1:40 for thou neiyidist wickidli to the Lord, and thin herte was ful of gile and of falsnesse.
2:1 Sone, neiyynge to the seruyce of God, stonde thou in riytfulnesse, and drede; and make redi thi soule to temptacioun.
2:2 Bere doun thin herte, and suffre, and bowe doun thin eere, and take the wordis of vndirstonding, and haaste thou not in to the tyme of deeth.
2:3 Suffre thou the susteynyngis of God; be thou ioyned to God, and abide thou, that thi lijf wexe in the last tyme.
2:4 Take thou alle thing that is set to thee, and suffre thou in sorewe, and haue thou pacience in thi lownesse.
2:5 For whi gold and siluer is preued in fier; forsothe men worthi to be resseyued ben preued in the chymeney of lownesse.
2:6 Bileue thou to God, and he schal rekeuere thee; and dresse thou thi weie, and hope thou in to hym. Kepe thou his drede, and wexe thou eld ther ynne.
2:7 Ye that dreden the Lord, abide his merci, and boowe ye not awei fro hym, lest ye falle doun.
2:8 Ye that dreden the Lord, bileue to hym, and youre mede schal not be auoidid.
2:9 Ye that dreden the Lord, hope into hym, and merci schal come to you into delityng.
2:10 Ye that dreden the Lord, loue hym, and youre hertis schulen be liytned.
2:11 Sones, biholde ye the naciouns of men, and wite ye, that no man hopide in the Lord, and was schent;
2:12 noon dwellide in hise heestis, and was forsakun; ether who inwardli clepide hym, and he despiside hym `that clepide?
2:13 For whi God is pitouse, and merciful, and he schal foryyue synnes in the dai of tribulacioun; and he is defendere to alle men, that seken hym in treuthe.
2:14 Woo to the `man with double herte, and with cursid lippis, and misdoynge hondys; and to a synnere entrynge in to the lond bi twei weies.
2:15 Wo to hem that ben dissolute of herte, that bileuen not to God; and therfor thei schulen not be defendid of him.
2:16 Wo to hem that han lost pacience, and that han forsake riytful weies, and han turned awei in to schrewid weies.
2:17 And what schulen thei do, whanne the Lord schal bigynne to biholde?
2:18 Thei that dreden the Lord, schulen not be vnbileueful to his word; and thei that louen hym, schulen kepe his weie.
2:19 Thei that dreden the Lord, schulen enquere tho thingis, that ben wel plesaunt to hym; and thei that louen him, schulen be fillid with his lawe.
2:20 Thei that dreden the Lord, schulen make redi her hertis, and schulen halewe her soulis in his siyt.
2:21 Thei that dreden the Lord, schulen kepe hise comaundementis, and schulen haue pacience til to the biholdyng of hym;
2:22 and schulen seie, If we doon not penaunce, we schulen falle in to the hondis of the Lord, and not in to the hondis of men.
2:23 For bi the greetnesse of hym, so and his merci is with hym.
3:1 The sones of wisdom ben the chirche of iust men, and the nacioun of hem is obedience and loue.
3:2 Dereworthe sones, here ye the doom of the fadir; and do ye so, that ye be saaf.
3:3 For whi God onouride the fadir in sones, and he sekith, and hath maad stidfast the doom of the modir in to sones.
3:4 He that loueth God, schal preie for synnes, and he schal absteyne hym silf fro tho, and he schal be herd in the preier of daies.
3:5 And as he that tresourith, so and he that onourith his modir.
3:6 He that onourith his fadir, schal be maad myrie in sones, and he schal be herd in the dai of his preier.
3:7 He that onourith his fadir, schal lyue bi lengere lijf; and he that obeieth to the fader, schal refreische the modir.
3:8 He that dredith the Lord, onourith fadir and modir; and he schal serue in werk, and word,
3:9 and al pacience to hem that gendriden hym as to lordis.
3:10 Onoure thi fadir, that the blessyng of God come to thee; and his blessing dwellith in the laste.
3:11 The blessyng of the fadir makith stidfast the housis of sones; but the cursyng of the modir drawith out the foundementis.
3:12 Haue thou not glorie in the dispisyng of thi fadir; for it is not glorie to thee, but confusioun.
3:13 For whi the glorie of a man is of the onour of his fadir; and the schenschip of the sone is a fadir with out onour.
3:14 Sone, resseyue the elde of thi fadir, and make thou not hym sori in his lijf;
3:15 and if he failith in wit, yyue thou foryyuenesse, and dispise thou not hym in thi vertu; for whi the almes of the fadir schal not be foryetyng.
3:16 For whi good schal be restorid to thee for the synne of the modir,
3:17 and bildyng schal be maad to thee in riytfulnesse; and it schal remembre of thee in dai of tribulacioun, and thi synnes schulen be releessid, as iys in clerenesse of the sunne.
3:18 He is of ful yuel fame, that forsakith the fadir; and he that wraththith the modir, is cursid of God.
3:19 Sone, performe thi werkis in myldenesse, and thou schalt be loued ouer the glorie of men.
3:20 In as myche as thou art greet, make thee meke in alle thingis, and thou schalt fynde grace bifore God; for whi the power of God aloon is greet,
3:21 and he is onourid of meke men.
3:22 Seke thou not hiyere thingis than thou, and enquere thou not strongere thingis than thou; but euere thenke thou tho thingis, whiche God comaundide to thee; and be thou not curiouse in ful many werkis of hym.
3:23 For it is not nedeful to thee to se with thin iyen tho thingis, that ben hid.
3:24 In superflu thingis nyle thou seke manyfold; and be thou not curiouse in many werkis of hym;
3:25 for whi ful many thingis aboue the wit of men ben schewid to thee.
3:26 For the suspecioun of many men hath disseyued hem, and withhelde her wittis in vanytee.
3:27 And hard herte schal haue yuel in the laste tyme; and he that loueth perel, schal perische ther ynne.
3:28 An herte that entrith bi tweie weies, schal not haue prosperitees, ether reste; and a man of schrewid herte schal be sclaundrid in tho.
3:29 A wickid herte schal be greuyd in sorewis; and a synnere schal `hepe to do synne.
3:30 Helthe schal not be to the synagoge of proude men; for whi the thicke wode of synne schal be drawun out bi the roote in hem, and it schal not be vndurstondun.
3:31 The herte of a wise man is vndurstondun in wisdom, and a good eere schal here wisdom with al coueitise.
3:32 A wijs herte and able to vndurstonde schal absteyne it silf fro synnes, and schal haue prosperitees in the werkis of riytfulnesse.
3:33 Watir quenchith fier brennynge, and almes ayenstondith synnes.
3:34 And God, the biholdere of hym that yeldith grace, hath mynde aftirward; and he schal fynde stidefastnesse in the tyme of his fal.
4:1 Sone, defraude thou not the almes of a pore man, and turne not ouere thin iyen fro a pore man.
4:2 Dispise thou not an hungri man, and wraththe thou not a pore man in his nedynesse.
4:3 Turmente thou not the herte of a nedi man, and tarie thou not the yifte to a man that is set in angwisch.
4:4 Caste thou not awei the preiyng of a man set in tribulacioun, and turne not awei thi face fro a nedi man.
4:5 Turne not awei thi iyen fro a pore man for ire, and yyue not occasioun to men axynge to curse thee byhynde.
4:6 For the preyer of hym that cursith thee in the bitternesse of soule, schal be herd; forsothe he that made hym, schal here hym.
4:7 Make thee eesi to speke to the congregacioun of pore men, and make meke thi soule to a preest, and make meke thin heed to a greet man.
4:8 Boowe doun with out sorewe thin eere to a pore man, and yelde thi debt, and answere thou pesibli in myldenesse.
4:9 Delyuere thou hym that suffrith wrong fro the hond of a proude man, and bere thou not heuyli in thi soule.
4:10 In demynge be thou merciful as a fadir to fadirles children, and be thou for an hosebonde to the modir of hem;
4:11 and thou schalt be as an obedient sone of the hiyeste, and he schal haue merci on thee more than a modir `hath merci on hir child.
4:12 Wisdom enspirith lijf to hise sones, and resseyueth men sekinge hym, and schal go bifore in the wei of riytfulnesse; and he that loueth that wisdom,
4:13 loueth lijf, and thei that waken to it, schulen biclipe the pesiblenesse, ether swetnesse, therof.
4:14 Thei that holden it, schulen enherite lijf; and whidir it schal entre, God schal blesse.
4:15 Thei that seruen it, schulen be obeiynge to the hooli; and God loueth hem, that louen it.
4:16 He that herith it, demeth folkis; and he that biholdith it, schal dwelle tristili.
4:17 If a man bileueth to it, he schal dwelle, and enherite it; and the creaturis of hem schulen be in confermyng.
4:18 For in temptacioun it goith with hym, and among the firste it chesith hym.
4:19 It schal brynge in on hym drede, and feer, and preuyng, and it schal turmente hym in the tribulacioun of his doctryn, til it tempte hym in hise thouytis, and bileue to his soule.
4:20 And it schal make hym stidefast, and schal brynge riyt weie to hym, and it schal make hym glad;
4:21 and schal make nakid hise priuytees to hym, and schal tresore on hym kunnyng, and vndurstondyng of riytfulnesse.
4:22 Forsothe if he errith, God schal forsake hym, and schal bitake hym in to the hondis of his enemy.
4:23 Sone, kepe thou tyme, and eschewe thou fro yuel.
4:24 Be thou not aschamed for thi lijf to seie treuthe; for whi ther is schame that bryngith synne,
4:25 and ther is schame that bryngith glorie and grace.
4:26 Take thou not a face ayens thi face, nethir a leesyng ayens thi soule.
4:27 Schame thou not thi neiybore in his fal,
4:28 nether withholde thou a word in the tyme of helthe. Hide not thi wisdom in the fairnesse therof;
4:29 for whi wisdom is knowun in tunge, and wit, and kunnyng, and techyng in the word of a wijs man; and stidfastnesse is in the werkis of riytfulnesse.
4:30 Ayenseie thou not the word of treuthe in ony maner; and be thou aschamed of the leesyng of thi mislernyng.
4:31 Be thou not aschamed to knouleche thi synnes; and make thee not suget to ech man for synne.
4:32 Nyle thou stonde ayens the face of the myyti, nethir enforse thou ayens the strok of the flood.
4:33 For riytfulnesse fiyte thou for thi soule, and til to the deth stryue thou for riytfulnesse; and God schal ouercome thin enemyes for thee.
4:34 Nyle thou be swift in thi tunge, and vnprofitable and slak in thi werkis.
4:35 Nyle thou be as a lioun in thin hous, turnynge vpsedoun thi meneals, and oppressynge hem that ben sugetis to thee.
4:36 Thin hond be not redi to take, and closid togidere to yyue.

5:2 Sue thou not the coueitise of thin herte in thi strengthe,
5:3 and seie thou not, As Y myyte, ether who schal make me suget for my dedis? For whi God vengynge schal venge.
5:4 Seie thou not, Y haue synned, and what sorewful thing bifelle to me? For the hiyeste is a pacient yeldere.
5:5 Of the foryyuenesse of synnes, nyle thou be without drede, nether heepe thou synne on synne.
5:6 And seie thou not, The merciful doyng of God is greet; he schal haue merci on the multitude of my synnes.
5:7 For whi merci and ire neiyeth soone fro hym, and his ire biholdith on synneris.
5:8 Tarie thou not to be conuertid to the Lord, and dilaie thou not fro dai in to dai.
5:9 For whi his ire schal come sodeynli, and he schal leese thee in the time of veniaunce.
5:10 Nyle thou be angwischid in vniust richessis; for tho schulen not profite in the dai of failing, ether of deth, and of veniaunce.
5:11 Wyndewe thee not in to ech wynd, and go thou not in to ech weie; for so a synnere is preued in double tunge.
5:12 Be thou stidfast in the weie of the Lord, and in treuthe and kunnyng of thi wit; and the word of pees and riytfulnesse sue thee perfitli.
5:13 Be thou mylde to here the word of God, that thou vndurstonde, and with wisdom brynge thou forth a trewe answere.
5:14 If thou hast vndirstondyng, answere thi neiybore; ellis thin hond be on thi mouth, lest thou be takun in a word vnwiseli tauyt, and be aschamed.
5:15 Onour and glorie is in the word of a wijs man; but the tunge of an vnprudent man is his distriyng.
5:16 Be thou not clepid a preuy yuel spekere in thi lijf, and be thou not takun in thi tunge, and be aschamed.
5:17 Schame and penaunce is on a theef, and worst schenschip is on a man of double tunge. Forsothe hatrede and enemytee and dispisyng is to a preuy bacbitere.
5:18 Iustifie thou a litil man and a greet man in lijk maner.
6:1 Nile thou for a freend be maad enemye to the neiybore; for whi an yuele man schal enherite vpbreidyng and dispisyng, and ech synnere enuyouse and double tungid.
6:2 Enhaunse thee not in the thouyt of thi soule, as a bole doith; lest thi vertu be hurtlid doun bi foli,
6:3 and it ete thi leeues, and leese thi fruytis, and thou be left as a drye tree in deseert.
6:4 Forsothe a wickid soule schal leese hym that hath it, and it yyueth hym in to the ioie of the enemye, and it schal leede forth in to the part of wickid men.
6:5 A swete word multiplieth frendis, and swagith enemyes; and a tunge wel graciouse schal be plenteuouse in a good man.
6:6 Many pesible men be to thee, and oon of a thousynde be a counselour to thee.
6:7 If thou hast a frend, haue hym in temptacioun, and bitake not liytli thi silf to hym.
6:8 For ther is a frend bi his time, and he schal not dwelle in the dai of tribulacioun.
6:9 And ther is a frend which is turned to enemytee; and ther is a freend, that schal schewe opynli hatrede, and chiding, and dispisyngis.
6:10 Forsothe ther is a frend, felowe of table, and dwellith not in the dai of nede.
6:11 If a frend dwellith stidfast, he schal be as a man euene with thee, and he schal do tristili in thi meyneal thingis.
6:12 If he mekith hym silf bifore thee, and hidith hym fro thi face, thou schalt haue good frendschip of oon acord.
6:13 Be thou departid fro thin enemyes, and take heede of thi frendis.
6:14 A feithful frend is a strong defendyng; forsothe he that fyndith him, fyndith tresour.
6:15 No comparisoun is to a feithful frend; weiyng of gold and of siluer is not worthi ayens the goodnesse of his feithfulnesse.
6:16 A feithful frend is medicyn of lijf, and of vndeedlynesse; and thei that dreden the Lord, schulen fynde hym.
6:17 He that dredith the Lord, schal haue euenli good frendschip; for whi his frend schal be at the licnesse of hym.
6:18 Sone, fro thi yongthe take thou doctryn, and til to hoor heeris thou schalt fynde wisdom.
6:19 As he that erith, and that sowith, neiye thou to it, and abide thou the goode fruytis therof.
6:20 For thou schalt trauele a litil in the werk therof, and thou schalt ete soone of the generaciouns therof.
6:21 Wisdom is ouer scharp to vntauyt men, and an hertles man schal not dwelle there ynne.
6:22 As the vertu of a stoon, preuyng schal be in hem; and thei schulen not tarie to caste awei it.
6:23 Forsothe the wisdom of techyng is bi the name therof, and it is not opyn to many men; but it dwellith with hem, of whiche it is knowun, til to the siyt of God.
6:24 Sone, here thou, and take the counsel of vndurstondyng, and caste thou not awei my counsel.
6:25 Set in thi foot in to the stockis therof, and thi necke in to the bies therof.
6:26 Make suget thi schuldir, and bere it, and be thou not anoied in the boondis therof.
6:27 In al thi wille go to it, and in al thi vertu kepe the weies therof.
6:28 Enquere thou it, and it schal be maad opyn to thee; and thou made holdinge wisdom forsake not it.
6:29 For in the laste thingis thou schalt fynde reste ther ynne, and it schal turne to thee in to deliting.
6:30 And the stockis therof schulen be to thee in defence of strengthe, and the foundementis of vertu, and the bie therof in a stoole of glorie.
6:31 For whi the fairnesse of lijf is in wisdom, and the boondis therof ben heelful byndyng.
6:32 Thou schalt `were it as a stoole of glorie, and thou schalt sette on thee a coroun of thankyng.
6:33 Sone, if thou takist heede to me, thou schalt lerne wisdom; and if thou yyuest thi wille, thou schalt be wijs.
6:34 If thou bowist doun thin eere, thou schalt take teching; and if thou louest for to here, thou schalt be wijs.

6:36 And if thou seest a wijs man, wake thou to hym, and thi foot trede on the greeces of his doris.
