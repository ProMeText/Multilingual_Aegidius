1:1 Poul, apostle, not of men, ne by man, but bi Jhesu Crist, `to the britheren that ben at Laodice,
1:2 grace to you, and pees of God the fadir, and of the Lord Jhesu Crist.
1:3 I do thankyngis to my God bi al my preier, that ye be dwelling and lastyng in him, abiding the biheest in the day of doom.
1:4 For neithir the veyn spekyng of summe vnwise men hath lettide you, the whiche wolden turne you fro the treuthe of the gospel, that is prechid of me.
1:5 And now hem that ben of me, to the profiyt of truthe of the gospel, God schal make disseruyng, and doyng benygnyte of werkis, and helthe of euerlasting lijf.
1:6 And now my boondis ben open, which Y suffre in Crist Jhesu, in whiche Y glade and ioie.
1:7 And that is to me to euerlastyng helthe, that this same thing be doon by youre preiers, and mynystryng of the Holi Goost, either bi lijf, either bi deeth.
1:8 Forsothe to me it is lijf to lyue in Crist, and to die ioie.
1:9 And his mercy schal do in you the same thing, that ye moun haue the same loue, and that ye be of oo will.
1:10 Therfore, ye weel biloued britheren, holde ye, and do ye in the dreede of God, as ye han herde the presence of me; and lijf schal be to you withouten eende.
1:11 Sotheli it is God that worchith in you.
1:12 And, my weel biloued britheren, do ye without eny withdrawyng what euer thingis ye don.
1:13 Joie ye in Crist, and eschewe ye men defoulid in lucre, `either foul wynnyng.
1:14 Be alle youre askyngis open anentis God, and be ye stidefast in the witt of Crist.
1:15 And do ye tho thingis that ben hool,
1:16 and trewe, `and chaast, and iust, and able to be loued; and kepe ye in herte tho thingis that ye haue herd and take;
1:17 and pees schal be to you.
1:18 Alle holi men greten you weel.
1:19 The grace of oure Lord Jhesu Crist be with youre spirit.
1:20 And do ye that pistil of Colocensis to be red to you.
