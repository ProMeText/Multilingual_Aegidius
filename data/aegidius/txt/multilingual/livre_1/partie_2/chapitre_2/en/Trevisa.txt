 Chapter IIFor it is schewed that moral vertues ben nought in
            naturel potencial vertues ot felyng, now it is to schewyng nou vertues
            bien distyngwed and hon thei bien in the intellectual inwit and in
            the appetit. thanne il is to distynguyng of vertues and of suche potencial
            vertues. For som vertues ben intellectual, and som moral, and somme
            mene bytwene intellectual and moral. the intellectual vertues bien in the invit that hatte
            intellectus speculatiuus, that is the schewing ot the inwit, and soche
            vertues bien speculatif sciens, as natural philosophi, gemetrie, and
            methaphisik, and other suche. And pure moral vertues bien in the appetite
            sensitif other intetlectif. And soche vertues bien iusticia, temporancia,
            fortitudo, mansuetudo and other soche, ot the nhiche ue schal trete
            syngulerlich. the mene vertues bytwene intellectual and moral beth in
            the inwyt, that hatte intellectus practicus, as prudencia and othere
            vertues pat longeth therto. the comentor super libris Ethicorum seith that
            prudencia is acounted a mene vertue bytwene moral vertues and intellectual
            vertues and may natheles De accounted with [mor]al vertues. But
            prudencia is not but in goode men as morel vertues beth not bote in gode men.
            Schrewed men, theigh thei mowe be kunnynge, falce and gylefole,
            thei more not be prudentes, as it schal be declared in the place
            therof. therfore, [vi] Ethicorum, it is iseid that it is inpossible te be
            prudens and not good
